📱 Fullscreen on iPhone

iPhone browsers can’t go fullscreen inside the page. For a no-address-bar game, add it to your home screen:

  1. Tap the Share button (a box with an ↑ arrow)
  2. Scroll down → Add to Home Screen
  3. Open Allama Road Run from your home screen

Tip: works best in Safari. Launched from the icon, it runs fullscreen automatically.
